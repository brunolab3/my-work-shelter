# Feature map

Where each part of the game lives, how it behaves and how to QA it. Read the section of what you touch; when you add or change a feature, update its entry here (AGENTS.md only keeps the rules).

## Startup

Startup: `./shelter` or the app `/Applications/work-shelter-public.app` (`./shelter instalar-app` → `scripts/install-app.mjs`, icon already prepared in `electron/icon/icon.png`; it opens `./shelter` with `SHELTER_DESDE_APP=1` and places the window itself). Install for people who don't clone: `install.sh` (`curl … | bash`: clones into `~/.work-shelter-public`, prepares and creates the app; running it again updates) (Electron + local server on 127.0.0.1:3778, under `server/supervisor.mjs`). Development: `npm run dev` (Vite on :5173 + server under the supervisor); to keep real data untouched, `SHELTER_DATOS=/temp/path` (and `SHELTER_PUERTO`). Identity on disk: `work-shelter-public` (Electron `userData`, `~/Library/Application Support/work-shelter-public`, `~/Library/Logs/work-shelter-public`, keychain service) and port 3778.

## New shelter

New shelter (`shared/initialShelter.ts`): only the service rooms, no example repos (nothing is cloned; `server/examples.ts` only recognises old example copies so that retiring or relinking such a room still sends the copy to the Trash). The welcome screen (`web/src/ui/Bridge.tsx`) asks for the repos root (`reposRoot`, pre-filled with `rootReposDefault()` in `server/state.ts`: `~/Sites`, `~/Developer`, `~/Projects`…). When the root is chosen (welcome, or changed later in the Vault; `takeRoot()` and `addRoom()` in `server/main.ts`), every git repo directly inside it becomes a room, plus wings (non-git folders with several repos inside, as at the Main Gate: `sugerencias` in `server/repos.ts`); rooms whose folder is outside the new root are retired, except those with someone working and the service rooms. With the root at `~` (or `/`) nothing is added automatically. Non-git folders keep showing as suggestions at the Main Gate. Groups (non-git root folders holding repos, such as `~/Fever/dice`) also offer their repos and projects, one level deep, at the Main Gate and in "Link to another folder", shown as `dice/bowie` (`sugerencias` sets `grupo`; `takeRoot()` skips them, the group itself still becomes a wing); the menus' 40-item cap counts only root folders (`suggestionItems` in `web/src/ui/RoomPanels.tsx`). A room is linked to another folder from its panel (action `vincularSala`) or retired. Integrations start `off` (`initialState`; no DEMO data (`server/integrations/demo.ts`, its English in `demoEnglish.ts`) and nothing read, not even Mac Mail/Calendar, GitHub via `gh` or Google via `gws`): entering the shelter opens the Vault to connect them; "forget" turns a source off again (`adapters.ts`, `integrations/index.ts`).

## Main Gate from GitHub

Main Gate «From GitHub…» (`GitHubList` and `CloneRepo` in `web/src/ui/RoomPanels.tsx`, `server/githubClone.ts`, actions `github.*` in `server/main.ts`): with the GitHub integration connected, your repos and your organisations' (`/user/repos`, up to 500, most recently pushed first) filtered as you type; owner/name or a URL can be pasted, with or without the integration. A repo already under the root (two levels, read from `.git/config`) or in a room is marked: its folder is admitted instead. Otherwise `git clone` (https, or ssh if `gh`'s `git_protocol` says so; your git credentials, no prompts) into an editable folder inside the root (a group subfolder too; not existing, not taken, not inside another repo), progress polled by the panel, Esc cancels; then the recruit sheet as usual. A finished clone goes into `ajustes.clonedFolders`, a trust boundary for `confianza()` in `server/sessions.ts`. QA: `server/githubClone.test.ts`, `server/trust.test.ts`.

## Game terminals

Game terminals: the PTYs live in `server/terminalHost.ts` (separate process, Unix socket) and survive server restarts; the client (`web/src/ui/Terminals.tsx`) behaves like iTerm: Esc and Ctrl+C always go to the program, kitty keyboard protocol. Terminals and intercoms are tabbed windows that minimise to the bar and maximise, with the same shortcuts (`web/src/ui/windows.tsx`, `Bar.tsx`). Many tabs: they keep a readable width and the strip scrolls; the ⋯ N button (⌘⇧A) lists them all to filter, go and close (one, selected, read or idle, a task's): in a terminal's strip, its tabs; for intercoms, at the end of the bar's COM row, every open intercom conversation by room and, in it, by whose it is: a header with their bust and name (a hologram's with its task in its color) over their tabs (`ComTabs` in `Dialog.tsx`; going to another's switches the intercom to it). Intercom tabs hold that dweller's or hologram's own conversations in the order they were opened, never moved by their state (the ⋯ N list too, grouped by room and then by talker: each where its first-opened tab is; `groupTabs` in `web/src/ui/tabOrder.ts`); the selector above the portrait (`WhoSelector` in `Dialog.tsx`; a chip like the bar's, always the portrait column's width, with a small bust: dwellers in their colors, holograms tinted with their task's color) opens one row per talker of the room in front (dweller or hologram), as the bar's hover list (`TalkerRow`; by their most urgent conversation's state as the tabs: waiting, unread, failed, working, ready; within a state, most recent conversation first, by the minute then by name; those without one last; dwellers before holograms; `talkerFirst` in `tabOrder.ts`); picking one brings their intercom to the front on their most urgent conversation (`goTab`), or just their intercom if they have none open; their tabs stay in their own strip and the ⋯ N list; states are coloured like their pilot light; closed, it flags the others with something for you (waiting, unread or failed). In the bar, intercoms go one chip per room (`comRooms` in `Bar.tsx`), compact: the room, its most urgent conversation's state and how many it has open (who and what in its title); the chips that don't fit go behind a «+N» chip at the row's end, whose hover or click lists them, room by room, one row per open panel as a chip's hover; the row is sticky: those left out stay out and the rest stay in their place when you switch conversations or their states change (the «+N» chip turns urgent instead), only the room in front is swapped in, for the least relevant one in view (by rank: waiting, failed, unread, then the rest; the last first), and those left out come back by rank where room frees up; with nothing before, the most urgent are kept in view (`overflowChips` in `tabOrder.ts`; the keyboard goes on into its list after the last chip in view). A chip opens the one with the most recent conversation and its hover, even with a single one, lists one row per open panel (the dweller and each hologram) as the selector above the portrait: their bust, name, most urgent conversation's state and when the latest of them last moved, without the conversations (those are the tabs), the one a click opens selected (`roomTalkers`, `TalkerRow`). Lists of conversations by recency (the finder, the logbook, a console's visitors) compare by the minute and then by name (`recentFirst` in `shared/recent.ts`), so they don't reshuffle while sessions work. Terminal tabs keep their opening order. QA: `scripts/qa-terminal.mjs --reinicio`, `scripts/qa-intercom.mjs`, `scripts/qa-tabs.mjs` (many tabs), `scripts/qa-bar.mjs`, `scripts/qa-com-bar.mjs` (many rooms, «+N») and `scripts/qa-holograms.mjs --estados/--nacer`, `scripts/qa-copies.mjs` (each holographic copy, with its intercom) (with the real game open, add `&res=0.5` to the URL).

## Shelter browsers

Shelter browsers (`web/src/ui/Browsers.tsx`, `<webview>` in `electron/main.cjs`): one website per window (Linear, in Planning; Outlook, Discord, Slack and GitHub, from the Communications notice board), to use them without leaving the game (integrations still bring the notices), no tabs or address bar, own persisted session (`persist:navegador`), confined to its domain (anything else goes to the system browser; a login popup opens inside the same browser, never in a window of its own); NAV row of the bar. The «Web» browser (`web`, `suelta`) shows any other https site a reply links (link options, below), in its own session (`persist:web`: not the work sites' logins, no notifications), confined to the domain it opened with (another domain gets a fresh `<webview>`). Electron only: changes to `electron/main.cjs` are applied by "restart". Discord and Slack use a Chrome user agent (`chrome: true`: with "Electron/44", Discord thinks it's "Chrome 44"). Gmail has no in-game browser (Google doesn't allow signing in from an embedded Chromium): its notices open in the system browser. QA: `scripts/qa-browser-sites.cjs` (each site loads without confinement cutting redirects), `scripts/qa-browser-board.mjs <cdp-port>` and `scripts/qa-browser.mjs <cdp-port>` (Electron with `SHELTER_DEV=1 SHELTER_CDP=<port>` and a temporary `--user-data-dir`). Spotify is not a `<webview>`: see Disco.

## Keyboard

Keyboard: two doors to everything, with its key visible in the top bar. Esc in the shelter opens the game menu (game things only: back, options, F1 shortcuts, restart and «Leave the shelter», whose hatch asks how: stay, go to the surface, everything to your phone or close and stop; `MenuGame` and `Salir` in `web/src/ui/Panels.tsx`; whatever opens from it returns to it with Esc). ⌘K, from anywhere (also while typing, in a terminal or a browser, which Electron forwards), opens the finder (`web/src/ui/Search.tsx`): open and recent conversations of every room (action `conversaciones.recientes`), actions with their key (most used first: "recurring") and, when typing, dwellers and rooms. A new shelter action is added to the finder's `acciones()`. QA: `scripts/qa-search.mjs`.

## Mouse in the shelter

Mouse in the shelter (`web/src/world/shelter.ts`, `apuntar()`): hovering a dweller, a copy, a device or the elevator shows its label with "Click", and a click uses it at once (opens its panel or the chat) while the Overseer walks there; whatever is in front wins. Holograms and helper robots show their full tag (name, task, detail) only while pointed at (the figure or its tag, `TagsLayer.at` in `web/src/world/labels.ts`; robots by `Helpers.at`) or, for a hologram, while the Overseer stands at it to talk (E); otherwise just their state's glyph (with the task color and ⏰), and idle ones nothing, so a crowded room stays readable. Each room marks the visible parts of its devices with `ctx.aparato(id, pieza)` (boxes measured before merging the static geometry, in `Interactive.zones`); unmeasured, the column behind its point counts. Dragging moves the camera (`ShelterCamera.desplazamiento`), which returns to the framing as soon as the Overseer walks. QA: `scripts/qa-aim.mjs`.

## Tasks and worktrees

Tasks and worktrees: talking to a room's dweller works in its main copy; worktrees belong to tasks (organise the room by tasks, `server/organize.ts`, and the Logistics split). Each Jira task (and PR) has a color (`shared/tasks.ts`, assigned and saved by `server/taskColors.ts`; in the client, `web/src/ui/tasks.ts`) shown on the boards, the screens, the hologram of its copy (body, projector disc, label) and its bust in the intercom and the bar; slug tasks and worktrees without a task all share the holograms' cyan, `NO_TASK_COLOR`, outside the palette (`hasTaskColor`), and take no assignment; the copy's name (label, bar, intercom, finder) adds the task's title when the Jira board has read it (`copyCaption` in `web/src/ui/copies.ts`). A task in a Jira epic (its `parent`, when that is an epic: `epicOf` in `server/integrations/jira.ts`) also shows a small diamond in the epic's own color, assigned and saved like a task's (`TasksColors.paint`), with the epic in its tooltip (`EpicMark`/`TaskMark` in `web/src/ui/windows.tsx`, `taskEpic` in `web/src/ui/tasks.ts`): tabs, the ⋯ N lists, the PR lists, the copy's tag and the Jira board, whose columns group tasks by epic under a header, those without one last with no header (`byEpic` in `shared/board.ts`). QA: `scripts/qa-epics.mjs`. One task, one worktree: creating a second one for a task that has one (`git worktree add`, `EnterWorktree`) is denied with the existing path (`oneWorktree` in `server/sessions.ts`, `taskWorktreeIn` in `shared/tasks.ts`). Task keys are Jira keys (ABC-123) or, outside Jira, descriptive slugs (`preflight-popover`), both matched exactly (`taskKey`, `worktreeHasTask` in `shared/tasks.ts`). Worktrees come from `send_to_room` with a task key, which creates one and opens a session in it: `git worktree add` from a session outside any worktree (a room's main copy) is denied (`handMadeWorktree` in `shared/tasks.ts`); `EnterWorktree` stays allowed, since it moves the session itself. `gh pr create` or `gh issue create` aimed at the original repo (`-R`, a remote, or a fork's parent) asks the user first, since nobody reads them there (`originalRepoGate` in `server/sessions.ts`, `ghCreateOf` in `shared/tasks.ts`).

## Logistics

Logistics (room `tareas`, `PLANNING_ROOM` in `shared/layout.ts`): plans a Jira task with every repo in view and splits it across rooms with in-process MCP tools (`server/dispatch.ts`), with a versioned shared plan (`server/plans.ts`) and the messenger robot (`web/src/world/messenger.ts`). Planning is the Jira board (`cruzarTablero` in `shared/board.ts`); dragging a card to another column (or ⇧←→) moves the task in Jira after a confirmation, only through a transition its workflow offers (`transitionsTo` in `shared/board.ts`, action `mover` in `server/integrations/jira.ts`, the only write to Jira). Its "Prod check" column, between "In review" and "Done", holds the tasks in the prod-check status picked for their board in the vault (production verification, below; matched by that setting, never by name, `prodCheckOf`): it is always shown, and a board without one has no transition into it. QA: `scripts/qa-move-jira.mjs`. Details: `docs/integrations-usage.md` ("Logistics, tasks and PRs").

## Messages between rooms

Messages between rooms (`server/roomNotices.ts`, MCP `rooms` in every conversation): `send_to_room` to any room's dweller, with or without a task key (with one, always a conversation with the task's history, framed as an outside request (`prompt.room.outside`): its open one, queued if busy (`ocupada`: starting, working or waiting) so it gets it when its current turn ends, and the tool result says «queued»; with none open, the last one in the task's worktree resumed (`resumeInFolder`, shared with `sendToFolder`); with none there, a new one; the tool result says which; without one, a new conversation in the main copy; the sender conversation's next messages to that room continue in it while it is open, and its replies back to the sender's room reach the sender conversation, both tracked per conversation and room in memory, never the sender's own; otherwise a conversation in a task worktree writing to another room carries its task's key (`worktreeOfSession` → `worktreeTask`) when that room already has the task's conversation or worktree, and the tool result says so; key and thread keep their own routing); inside the same room the dweller hands it to the task's hologram (`delivered` in `web/src/world/messenger.ts`), or the messenger robot does when he can't (talking with the Overseer, as when he sends it mid-conversation; waiting); to another room, the robot runs there down the lift shaft. QA: `scripts/qa-messenger.mjs`. With a `thread` slug instead of a key, to that thread's open conversation in the room's main copy (queued if busy; a new one takes over once it closes, and never the sender's own), tracked per room and thread in memory: "Restart the game" closes every conversation anyway. Over the limit of 3 messages a minute from one room to another, or while earlier over-limit ones still wait, the message becomes a scheduled one (`Scheduled.defer`, flagged `overLimit`: same room, key or thread, delivered verbatim with today's framing and routing, no weekend hold) for when the window frees up, spaced so they don't hit it again and swept at that moment (`wake`); they show in the ⏰ lists, go out in order (one still over the limit holds the next ones, retried without a warning), and count towards the conversation's 10, with the old error when that list is full. Conversations in a worktree also get `request_worktree_cleanup`: the game writes the cleanup request from the session's cwd (worktree, branch, merge into `local` if the repo has that branch) and sends it through the own room's `cleanup` thread, one cleanup at a time. Details: `docs/integrations-usage.md` ("Logistics, tasks and PRs").

## Scheduled messages

Scheduled messages (`server/scheduled.ts`, pure scheduling in `shared/scheduled.ts`; tools `schedule_message`, `list_scheduled`, `cancel_scheduled` in the MCP `rooms`): a dweller leaves a message for later ("let's review it on Monday") to its own conversation (resumed by its `claudeId` in its worktree, plan mode, if it was closed) or to a room like `send_to_room`; delivered on time even after restarts (checked every minute and on start; late ones are marked), saved in `DATOS/mensajes-programados.json`. A day without a time is 09:00 local; weekends are held to Monday 09:00; at most 10 per conversation and 60 days ahead. Shown in the intercom under the portrait (`ScheduledMenu` in `Dialog.tsx`: ✎ edit, ✕ cancel): with a conversation open, the ones it scheduled; resuming one, that one's; from the menu, every one of that room or hologram even with its conversation closed (`placeOf`/`ofPlace`: a conversation target by room and the worktree of its cwd, a room target by room and the worktree of its key). The shelter shows ⏰ with the next one's time on that dweller's or hologram's tag (`scheduledMark` in `web/src/world/labels.ts`). Also in ⌘K and the Calendar (message `programados`). QA: schedule a message from a worktree conversation, close it, and check the hologram's ⏰ and its intercom menu. Details: `docs/integrations-usage.md`.

## Resuming after the usage limit

A turn the plan's usage limit stops (a `rate_limit_event` with status `rejected`, not on extra usage, or an assistant `rate_limit` error, then the turn's error result or its end) is not an error (`limitStop` in `server/sessions.ts`): the conversation goes idle with `limit: { resetsAt, resumeAt }` in its summary and the intercom says when it continues (`limitLine` in `Dialog.tsx`). `server/limitResume.ts` queues it (`DATOS/reanudar-limite.json`, so it survives "Restart the game") and resumes one every half-minute round once due: the reset plus 2 min, most recently stopped first, 4 min apart; a new `rejected` pushes the whole queue after its reset; a message from the Overseer takes it out of the queue. It resumes the open conversation, or by its `claudeId` in its folder with its own permission mode (never `bypassPermissions`), prompt `prompt.limitResume`. On start it also picks up conversations of the rooms' folders and worktrees whose transcript (last 3 days) ends in the limit error (`scanStopped`): those stopped before this existed or whose queue was lost. QA: `server/limitResume.test.ts`.

## Reviews of your PRs

Reviews of your PRs (`server/prReviews.ts`, every minute with GitHub connected): what others write on your open PRs goes to the conversation in the PR's worktree. Details: `docs/integrations-usage.md` ("Logistics, tasks and PRs").

## CI failures of your PRs

CI failures of your PRs (`server/ciFailures.ts`, every minute): a failing check on a PR's head goes once to the same conversation as reviews. Details: `docs/integrations-usage.md` ("Logistics, tasks and PRs").

## Finished tasks

Finished tasks (`server/taskCleanup.ts`, every 5 minutes with GitHub connected): when all PRs of a task in a room's repo are merged or closed, the game removes its worktrees and local branches by itself if git shows nothing left (uncommitted changes to tracked files, stash of its branch, commits not on the remote nor on the PR's head; untracked files don't count and go to the macOS Trash); with an open conversation it waits; with work left it asks the Overseer once with a notice that stays until answered (`NoticeQuestion`, action `aviso.responder`) before forcing it (a yes while a conversation is open in it removes it as soon as that conversation closes). A worktree removed some other way (by hand, or by a session after merging) takes its conversations with it: `orphanedConversations` closes them in the minute sweep of `server/main.ts` once idle, read and not in use in the intercom (`enUso`); until then its hologram stays with them (the client's fallback for a session's fresh worktree in `web/src/world/holograms.ts`). QA: unit test in `server/taskCleanup.test.ts`.

## Pre-flight of a task

Pre-flight of a task (`web/src/ui/Preflight.tsx`, model `shared/preflight.ts`, `server/preflight.ts`): whether each PR of a Jira task, across rooms, is ready to merge (state, CI, review, conflicts, blockers; a click opens the PR), in a small floating box over whatever is open (not a panel: Esc or a click outside closes it and nothing underneath changes), also for tasks already fully merged; offered (intercom ✈, ⌘K, F on the selected card of the deploy board and of the Jira board) only for tasks with a PR (`taskHasPRs`), plus the ask-first gate on `git push` (`pushGate` in `server/sessions.ts`) with compare and checklist, which the window doesn't show. Details: `docs/integrations-usage.md` ("Pre-flight of a task"). QA: `scripts/qa-preflight.mjs`.

Production verification of a task (model and state machine `shared/verification.ts`, saved per task in `DATOS/verificaciones.json` by `server/verifications.ts`; plan: `plans/2026-11-17-production-verification.md`, phases 1 and 2): the task's dweller proposes the plan before the merge with `verification_plan` (MCP `rooms`, `server/roomNotices.ts`): checkpoints, one per PR (optional) and a final one (required), each with signals (`must-disappear`, `must-appear`, `must-run`, `no-new-errors`; where to look in the observability connector's own terms, the baseline, a 24 h default deadline). The Overseer approves it at the bottom of the pre-flight (action `verificacion.aprobar`; a changed plan is a new version that needs approving again), which also shows each checkpoint's state. After the deploy the dweller records evidence with `verification_report`: waiting-deploy → live (per service) → waiting-traffic → evidenced | failed, or inconclusive past the deadline with no traffic (checked on read), then wait more or accept with a reason, on the Overseer's word. The game queries nothing itself. Every five minutes (`repasar`, `server/main.ts`) it opens the checkpoint of a merged PR and, once every PR of the task is merged and their checkpoints are settled (or when the tracker has the task in its board's prod-check status), the final one; the deadline counts from there (no deploy by then is inconclusive too). It keeps one recheck scheduled per open checkpoint in the conversation that proposed the plan (`Scheduled.scheduleGame` in `server/scheduled.ts`, in its ⏰ list; 30 min, 2 h, then each morning; if its worktree is gone, a new conversation in the room with the plan in the message), drops it when the checkpoint closes, tells the Overseer once when one closes or runs out of time, and an inconclusive one also asks its conversation to ask the Overseer. The tracker's side (`server/verificationBoards.ts`): each board (a Jira project or a Linear team, the key's prefix) has its own workflow, so its prod-check and done statuses are picked from its own statuses in the tracker's page of the vault (`BoardStatusesView` in `web/src/ui/Vault.tsx`, `ajustes.verificacionTableros`; Jira's project statuses, Linear's team states, action `estadosTablero`), and the game asks once at start for the boards of open tasks still without one; only connected trackers count. The pre-flight shows the task's tracker status and, once its final checkpoint is evidenced, offers the workflow's own transition to the board's done status (Jira only, action `verificacion.cerrar`). The deploy board's merged cards and the task's hologram label show its 🔭 state once a checkpoint opened (`verificationMark` in `web/src/ui/tasks.ts`, message `verificaciones`). Not yet: the Watchtower room. QA: `shared/verification.test.ts`, `server/verifications.test.ts`, `server/verificationBoards.test.ts`.

## Usage per conversation

Usage per conversation: the session summary carries `tokens` and `contexto` (`shared/protocol.ts`); `Usage` in `web/src/ui/usage.tsx` shows it in the intercom and in the room card (`RoomCard` in `web/src/ui/Panels.tsx`). In the intercom's status line, an idle conversation (`compactNeed`) shows «Compact» from 150k tokens of context, pulsing from 300k (or 40 % / 60 % of a smaller window; `compactLevel` in `shared/compact.ts`; the bar's colours keep their own levels, `contextLevel`): it sends `/compact` as a message, and the `compact_boundary` that follows logs it and measures the context again (`server/sessions.ts`). Dwellers auto-compact at the vault's «Auto-compact at» window (default 400k, Claude Code compacts ~33k below it; 0 = Claude Code's own, near the whole 1M window), passed as the `autoCompactWindow` flag setting in `optionsBase`; it applies to conversations launched afterwards. A message the Overseer didn't type (another room, a scheduled one, a review or CI failure: `ctxPlan.notify` → `sessions.deliver`) to an idle open conversation past 300k sends `/compact` first.

## Links in replies

Links in replies (`web/src/ui/markdown.ts`): a left click opens it the first of its ways (inside the game when it can; a file of the Mac asks `enlace.tipo` first, and a missing one shows the list with a warning), a right click opens a small list anchored at the link with all of them (`web/src/ui/linkOptions.ts`, options in `shared/linkOptions.ts`), the first one marked and taken with Enter, ↑↓ to move, Esc or a click elsewhere closes it; links inside pages of the «Página» window behave the same (`server/page/notes.js` posts them to the game, `pageLinkTarget` resolves a relative one next to the page and ignores anything but files of the Mac, web and mail; the list opens at the pointer); never a new window of the game. Web links: «Open in the game» (Electron and https only: the site's own shelter browser, or else the «Web» one; a page another local program serves, in the «Página» window), «Open in browser» (system browser), «Copy link» (mail links: mail app, copy address). Files of the Mac (action `enlace.tipo`, `linkKind`: what they open in, without opening them): «Open in the game» for pages and images, then where the Mac opens them (`enlace.local` with `fuera`: default app, Preview, editor, Finder for folders), «Reveal in Finder», «Copy path»; a missing one only offers copying its path. Thumbnails open their image in the game directly. QA: `scripts/qa-link-options.mjs`. Those pointing at a file on the Mac (`/Users/…`, `~/…`, `file://…`, and the temporary folders `/tmp`, `/private/tmp`, `/var/folders`) are opened by the server without running anything (action `enlace.local`, `openLink` in `server/localPages.ts` and `openLocal` in `server/open.ts`: HTML pages (plans, reports, compares) and Markdown files (rendered to HTML by the server, mermaid blocks drawn) inside the game, in the «Página» window of `Browsers.tsx`: served at `/pagina/<key>/…` with a random key per folder, a `sandbox` CSP header and a sandboxed `<iframe>`, no data requests (`connect-src 'none'`; only scripts, styles and fonts from the public CDNs jsdelivr, unpkg and cdnjs, which the dwellers' pages use for marked or mermaid); the Overseer leaves notes on them (select → «✎ Note», `server/page/notes.js` injected in each page, saved per file in the client, `pageNotes`) and sends them inline to the conversation that linked the page (every conversation has the `compare_changes` tool, `server/compare.ts`: its branch vs. its base as such a page, or another worktree of the same repo with `carpeta`, in the game's `.data/compares`, to review with notes before a push); ## Always up to date, without asking the Overseer for anything

Always up to date, without asking the Overseer for anything (`scripts/fingerprints.cjs`, content fingerprints of client, server and app; `scripts/huellas.cjs` is its legacy name for copies started before the English rename): "restart" rebuilds the client if its fingerprint doesn't match the last build's and, if `electron/`, the supervisor or `./shelter` changed, exits with code 76 and the app reopens entirely (terminals alive). An app opened with an Electron other than the one in `node_modules` reinstalls itself (`install-app.mjs`) and reopens. Opening the app clears the game's HTTP cache and, if an older server is still running, brings it up to date (`/poner-al-dia`). The server serves assets with ETag and `no-cache` (only hashed builds are immutable). Never ask the Overseer to clear caches, reopen or reinstall the app to see changes: if something doesn't update by itself, fix it in the game.
